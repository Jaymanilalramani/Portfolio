A little bit about me

Jay Manilal Ramani

Frontend & Full-Stack Developer · AI/ML Enthusiast

Dhule, Maharashtra, India

I'm a Computer Engineering undergraduate at SVKM's Institute of Technology, Dhule, working towards my B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University). I started with the fundamentals — C, C++, data structures and OOP — and grew into building complete products on the web.

I care about clean code, accessible interfaces and shipping things that actually get used.
